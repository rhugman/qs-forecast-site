About this forecast what it is, what drives it, what it is not

Probabilistic forecasts of the groundwater level at SNIRH monitoring wells of the Querença-Silves aquifer (Algarve). One statistical model per well, fitted to the measured levels and driven by daily rain, evapotranspiration and pumping, run forward on ensembles of ECMWF weather forecasts. Issued weekly; two seasons a year, 1 April to 30 September and 1 October to 31 March.

Weather members: loading Members, spread and cumulative rain against past years.

Forcing: loading

Verified wells passed a 25-year hindcast test against the well's own seasonal norm (see Skill); the others are shown with the same forecast but carry no skill claim.

Research demonstration, non-commercial and unofficial. The forecasts are probabilistic model output with measurements that may be months old; they are not a decision product and carry no warranty.
