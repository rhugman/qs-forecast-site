Hindcast skill

Walk-forward hindcast at every candidate site, origins 1 April and 1 October 2001-2025, parameters refit before each origin, ESP weather members (resampled observed years) and the observed weather as the known-forcing ceiling. Scores are against the site's own head climatology: CRPSS above zero means the forecast beats it.

The 1 April conditional correction

A leave-one-origin-year-out regression of the season-end error, and of its spread, on the state known at the origin (the winter's rain, the Arade storage, the footprint level, the anchor's own residual and percentile). It is applied to the published band at the western net-pumping sites for an issue between 1 April and the season end, and nowhere else. The gate above is on the uncorrected band.
